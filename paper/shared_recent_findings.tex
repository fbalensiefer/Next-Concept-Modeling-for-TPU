\subsection{Matched maintenance ablations and fresh source distributions}
The historical 20-model stability result above remains conditional on its
original domain. A subsequent 100-run paired ablation reused seeds 13--17,
400 codec updates and 200 decoder replay updates per model. It compared
ordinary EMA without recovery, ordinary EMA with recovery, the original
slowed-centroid reference, and fixed coarse binary endpoints. Every condition
retained the same data-driven initialization, entropy objective, temperature
and commitment schedules, neural optimizer and four objective arms. Thus it
isolates centroid movement/recovery, not the necessity of all regularizers.
\RecentMaintenanceTable
Unguarded EMA reconstructed perfectly but failed the usage-window criterion
in 7/20 runs. Recovery and fixed endpoints were promising within that envelope;
small CPU timing differences did not establish substantial acceleration.

A separately specified follow-up used fresh student seeds 31--35, six full-support
binary laws and lengths four or eight: the original constant-mode source,
independent Bernoulli(0.2) symbols, a persistent Markov chain, noisy parity,
skewed constant modes and alternating modes. All three stable quantizers were
trained separately on each source with the same 512/512/256 training,
validation and calibration draw budgets. Six independently initialized small
teachers supplied alignment features; the analytic source law, rather than the
fitted teacher law, supplied training samples and population evaluation.
Consequently these TV values are not direct before/after comparisons with the
historical teacher-law pilot. All 360 models received 400 codec and 200 replay
updates. The study is broader-distribution mechanism validation, not
natural-language evaluation or transfer to an unseen domain.
\RecentDomainTable
No condition passed every fidelity check. The fixed binary condition incurred
11/120 model failures, EMA with recovery 16/120 and reference 17/120; those
counts do not imply a uniform winner, since failure severity differed. In
noisy-parity seed 31, AD fixed endpoints reconstructed 25\% of source mass and
missed 75\% under the factorized oracle. The checks require sequence accuracy
at least 98\%, missing/outside mass at most 1\%, and collision-based fidelity
ceiling at least 98\%. Balanced coarse usage is diagnostic, not a universal

\subsection{Empirical dependency models on frozen reference codecs}
The next experiment fitted four tabular priors to the original 512 training
code observations of each of the 120 reference codecs: independent fine codes,
adjacent pairs, a conditional dependence tree and a joint fine-level table.
No neural or codec update was performed. Every conditional row receives total
symmetric Dirichlet pseudo-count one. Tree topology uses empirical conditional
mutual information from training codes only and is rooted at a center. This
is related to Chow--Liu approximation~\citep{chow1968}; the shared topology and
smoothed tables are not asserted to retain its unrestricted optimality guarantee.

A sampled fidelity filter requires validation sequence accuracy and training
collision ceiling at least 98\%. It flags 16 codecs and accepts 104, including
one subsequently found below the population reconstruction threshold. The
filter is not a population certificate. All 480 prior fits and all-codec
results remain archived; population probabilities are used only for evaluation,
not fitting, topology, smoothing selection or the eligibility filter.
\RecentPriorTable
Joint tables improve mean decoded TV over independent tables on every source.
On Markov data, the conditional tree instead beats the joint table, consistent
with a beneficial sparsity/estimation tradeoff. At length eight the independent,
pair, tree and joint families have 17, 25, 31 and 511 free parameters including
the coarse distribution. Independent, pair and joint sampling take two rounds;
observed center-rooted trees take three to six. Enumeration/fit timings are not
end-to-end generation latency. Full joint tables grow exponentially and remain
finite-domain controls, not a proposed general language architecture.

\subsection{Exact innovation coordinates and the alignment-target diagnosis}
For the tested symmetric binary Markov law, define
\begin{equation}
 y_1=x_1,\qquad y_t=x_t\oplus x_{t-1}\quad(t>1).
\end{equation}
The variables are independent, with probabilities 0.5 for the initial bit and
0.15 for subsequent flip indicators. Prefix XOR reconstructs every input.
For the noisy-parity law (90\% uniform even parity plus 10\% uniform), let
\begin{equation}
 y_i=x_i\ (i<L),\qquad y_L=\bigoplus_{i=1}^{L}x_i.
\end{equation}
The first variables are independent fair bits and the last is an independent
Bernoulli(0.05) error bit. Both are bijections; exhaustive enumeration verifies
exact reconstruction and factorization to floating-point roundoff. All latent
bits can be drawn independently, followed by deterministic decoding. Prefix
XOR admits linear work and logarithmic parallel depth via associative
scan~\citep{blelloch1993}. These are classical known-structure constructions,
not newly learned transforms or universal independence theorems.

For any finite bijection \(T\), distributional KL is invariant:
\begin{equation}
 D_{\rm KL}\!\left(P_X\middle\|(T^{-1})_\#q\right)
 =D_{\rm KL}(P_{T(X)}\|q).
\end{equation}
This removes codec collision error and makes representation choice directly
relevant to factorization. Arbitrary finite distributions need not admit
independent fixed-length coordinates under a deterministic bijection, and an
inverse can itself be costly. These simple sources therefore motivate
information-preserving baselines without establishing a solution for language.

A separate replayable diagnostic examines the historical alignment target.
The teacher sees shifted causal inputs, whereas the codec sees the full text.
Its complete projected target is identical for sequences differing only in
the final byte, although that byte retains 0.4293 conditional bits. PCA/head
reconstruction changes teacher predictive probabilities by only
\(2.33\times10^{-6}\) mean KL nats; alignment substantially improves feature
matching but cannot make this target information-complete. This identifies a
conditioning conflict, not a controlled causal explanation of all failures.

\subsection{Evidence scope, reproducibility and next steps}
The 480 fits reuse 120 codecs; objective arms share seeds and datasets. They
are not 480 independent replications. Paired analysis should treat student
seeds as replication units. The broader studies adapt separately to each
small binary source and retain the historical alignment convention and shared
regularizers. No pretrained transfer, language quality or TPU result follows.
Reconstruction constraints and uncertainty-aware acceptance should precede
quantizer replacement; sparse dependencies and small joint blocks are the
next tractable modeling controls.
