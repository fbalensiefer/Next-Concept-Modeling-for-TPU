\begin{abstract}
SCALE (Size-Consistent Architecture and Latent Exchange) formulates an adaptive
compiler from pretrained autoregressive Transformers to hierarchical generative
models: calibrate functional representations, design a rate- and dependence-aware
code hierarchy, then reuse compatible operators or perform bounded adaptation.
Conditional transfer and logit-error bounds, the conditional-total-correlation
limit of factorized priors, and capacity/identifiability counterexamples delimit
what such a compiler can guarantee. The full pretrained, cross-size compiler
remains a proposal. Completed local mechanism studies now use one small trained
teacher and an exactly enumerable 16-sequence domain. Four-arm controls separated
alignment from dependence, diagnosed a code-collision reconstruction ceiling,
and tested balanced codec sampling and deterministic coarse-codebook maintenance.
All \StabilityModels{} new-seed models passed fixed hard-stability and coverage
gates, with minimum coarse perplexity \StabilityPP{} of 2; the reliable
teacher-alignment gate still failed. The companion JAX/Flax system records
\TestCases{} passing CPU tests, resumable training, seeded generation and CPU
inference measurements. These milestones validate a reproducible mechanism-study
platform and a small-domain repair, not pretrained transfer, general language
quality, adaptation savings or TPU performance.
\end{abstract}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} cross-architecture transfer; next-concept modeling;
residual vector quantization; conditional total correlation; distillation; JAX/Flax.

\section{Introduction}
Pretrained autoregressive Transformers contain capabilities acquired at high
training cost~\citep{vaswani2017attention,biderman2023}. Reusing these capabilities
in a different generative factorization is appealing, but tensor copying alone
cannot resolve width changes, basis symmetries, incompatible operators or
conditional independence assumptions. Function-preserving transformations,
stitching, merging and distillation provide partial precedents rather than a
universal conversion theorem~\citep{chen2016net2net,hinton2015,ainsworth2022,chen2025stitch,wang2024mamba}.

Next-Concept Modeling (NCM) predicts multiscale discrete codes coarse-to-fine,
with positions within a level generated in parallel~\citep{kumbong2026ncm}.
Reconstruction can remain good while codes at the same parallel step retain
strong dependence. A useful compiler must therefore co-design its
representation and its generative factorization. SCALE proposes to align
functional representations into a calibrated intermediate space, design a
hierarchy for fidelity and predictable dependence, and transplant parameters
only when an operator-level equivalence is justified.

This revision separates three evidence levels. The mathematical statements are
conditional results. The full external pretrained compiler remains proposed.
The local NCM system and a sequence of finite-domain studies have been
implemented and evaluated. Those studies first exposed coverage failures, then
repaired hard coarse-codebook stability, but did not establish a reliable
teacher-alignment benefit. The systems companion~\citep{systemscompanion}
describes implementation contracts and uses the same empirical records; its
results are not independent replications of this paper.

Contributions are a falsifiable compiler contract and conditional analysis;
a completed paired-arm mechanism-study platform; an explicit collision-based
failure diagnosis; and reproducible coverage/stability interventions with
negative alignment results. No universal lossless compression, pretrained
cross-size conversion or adaptation-compute reduction is claimed.

\section{Related Work and Novelty Boundary}
\paragraph{Functional correspondence.}
Language-model stitching and cross-size feature transplantation motivate
calibration across widths~\citep{chen2025stitch}. CKA is a useful descriptive
similarity measure, not a functional-equivalence certificate~\citep{kornblith2019}.
MATCHA models many-to-many layer correspondences explicitly~\citep{sudakov2026}.
These precedents motivate alignment; they do not establish that a single
low-dimensional interface preserves a teacher's generation law.

\paragraph{Weight and architecture transformations.}
Net2Net gives function-preserving changes under specific architectures and
operators~\citep{chen2016net2net}. Permutation-aware merging, unequal-size merging
and heterogeneous fusion constrain rather than eliminate compatibility
requirements~\citep{ainsworth2022,soro2026,chen2026fusion}. Cross-architecture
compression and Transformer-to-state-space adaptation similarly require measured
adaptation~\citep{cui2026,wang2024mamba}. Distillation and restricted low-rank
updates are candidate fallback mechanisms~\citep{hinton2015,hu2022lora}.

\paragraph{Discrete parallel generation.}
VQ-VAE and residual codecs supply discrete representations and EMA mechanisms
\citep{oord2017vqvae,zeghidour2021soundstream}; NCM supplies the principal target
factorization~\citep{kumbong2026ncm}. ResGen explores another hierarchical
sampling design~\citep{kim2025resgen}. The conditional-TC factorization penalty
below is a known information identity, also discussed by Wen et al. in the
context of parallel sampling~\citep{wen2026parallel}. SCALE's proposed
contribution is to use such constraints in an adaptive compiler and falsifiable
allocation policy, not to claim that identity, NCM or model stitching as new.

\section{Problem and Compiler Contract}
Let teacher size \(s\) identify a model with weights \(\mathcal W_s\), hidden width
\(d_s\), hidden functions \(h_s\) and output law \(p_s(x\mid u)\) under a fixed
prompt and decoding protocol. A compiler would map
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal C(\mathcal W_s,\mathcal A,\mathcal B_{\rm res},\xi)
   \longmapsto (Q_\psi,d_\psi,p_\theta,J_s),
\end{equation}
where \(\mathcal A\) is calibration data, \(\mathcal B_{\rm res}\) is a target
resource envelope, \(\xi\) fixes randomness and \(J_s\) is an audit certificate.
The certificate must distinguish proved compatibility, measured approximation
and untested assumptions, and account for calibration, search, codec, prior and
adaptation costs. It is not implemented in full by the current experiment logs.

For fixed geometry the target code law factorizes as
\begin{equation}
 p_\theta(i\mid u)=\prod_{k=1}^{K}\prod_{j=1}^{n_k}
 p_\theta(i_{k,j}\mid i_{<k},u).
 \label{eq:scalefactor}
\end{equation}
A probabilistic token decoder induces
\begin{equation}
 p_{\theta,\psi}(x\mid u)=\sum_i p_\theta(i\mid u)d_\psi(x\mid i,u).
 \label{eq:studentlaw}
\end{equation}
The local studies instead use deterministic byte argmax for \(d_\psi\); their
exact TV is specific to that decoding rule. Prompts are empty and output length
is fixed. A shared representation width is a design choice, not evidence of
size consistency or a lossless teacher-independent coordinate system.

\section{Proposed Six-Stage Architecture and Current Implementation}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Audit teacher and budget.} Fix weights, tokenizer, layers, decoding,
calibration splits, resource envelope and accounting rules before adaptation.
The local platform records one synthetic teacher and fixed data hashes.
\item \textbf{Calibrate representations.} Seek functionally relevant maps
\(P_s:\R^{d_s}\to\R^r\), with optional injections \(R_s:\R^r\to\R^{d_s}\).
Cross-size maps may use stitching, many-to-many layers or task losses.
The completed studies use a sign-fixed PCA map for one teacher; this is neither
learned cross-teacher alignment nor a verified operator equivalence.
\item \textbf{Design the code hierarchy.} Choose \((K,n_k,V,D)\) to balance
fidelity, nominal rate, conditional TC and serial depth. The current studies fix
these shapes and evaluate exact small-support dependence; adaptive architecture
search is future work.
\item \textbf{Construct a level prior.} Train the shared Transformer on frozen
codec labels, preserving earlier-level conditioning. This stage is implemented,
as are stochastic code sampling and deterministic byte decoding.
\item \textbf{Reuse or adapt operators.} Transplant only certified compatible
maps; otherwise allow restricted distillation, adapters or capacity expansion.
No selective pretrained operator transplant has been demonstrated.
\item \textbf{Evaluate and allocate.} Audit hard usage, coverage, output law,
quality and complete adaptation cost; reject or expand targets when gates fail.
Local frozen protocols and exact diagnostics are implemented. Automated
allocation, scalable density estimation and general compiler certificates
remain proposed.
\end{enumerate}
The distinction between a stage's executable local mechanism and its full
cross-size form is essential: the local PCA/code studies do not complete SCALE.

\section{Conditional Guarantees and Failure Cases}
\subsection{Exact transfer on an invariant compatible subspace}
\begin{proposition}
Let \(R_s\in\R^{d_s\times r}\) have full column rank and
\(P_sR_s=I_r\). If a teacher operator \(F_s\) and canonical operator \(M\)
satisfy \(F_s(R_sz)=R_sM(z)\) for every reachable \(z\), then
\(P_sF_sR_s=M\) on that set. If an entire stack has compatible interfaces and
heads satisfying these conditions, its canonical composition is exact there.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Apply \(P_s\) to the assumed equality and use \(P_sR_s=I_r\). Repeating this
identity across compatible reachable interfaces gives the composition result.
\end{proof}
Nonlinear attention, normalization and residual branches must individually
satisfy the compatibility conditions or be included in the certified operator.
A dimension match, high CKA or successful PCA reconstruction is insufficient.
The present experiments did not verify these premises for a pretrained model.

\subsection{A local output-error bound}
\begin{proposition}
Suppose \(h_s=R_sz+\eta_s\), \(P_sR_s=I_r\), and a reconstructed canonical
vector obeys \(\|\widehat z-P_sh_s\|\leq\epsilon_Q\). Let a compatible logit
map \(g\) be \(L_g\)-Lipschitz into the infinity norm, with teacher logit error
at most \(\epsilon_T\) relative to \(g(z)\) and student logit error at most
\(\epsilon_D\) relative to \(g(\widehat z)\). Then their logit vectors satisfy
\begin{equation}
 \|\ell_s-\ell_{\theta,\psi}\|_\infty\leq
 \Delta=\epsilon_T+L_g(\|P_s\|\,\|\eta_s\|+\epsilon_Q)+\epsilon_D,
\end{equation}
and the KL between their softmax laws is at most \(2\Delta\).
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Since \(P_sh_s=z+P_s\eta_s\), the canonical error is at most
\(\epsilon_Q+\|P_s\|\|\eta_s\|\). Apply Lipschitz continuity and the triangle
inequality. Log-sum-exp is 1-Lipschitz in the infinity norm, so each log-ratio of
the two softmax laws is at most \(2\Delta\); average under the teacher law.
\end{proof}
This is a one-interface bound with common conditioning and a compatible logit
head. It is not a certified free-running sequence bound. A sequential chain
rule would require uniform conditional control on all relevant teacher prefixes;
a hierarchical mixture needs its own coupling or output-law argument. None of
these constants has been certified for the local or proposed pretrained models.

\subsection{Conditional total correlation limits parallel factorization}
\begin{proposition}
For a fixed discrete law of \((I_{<k},I_k)\), the best unrestricted within-level
factorized conditional approximation satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \min_{\{q_j\}}\mathbb E_{I_{<k}}
 D_{\rm KL}\!\left(P(I_k\mid I_{<k})\middle\|\prod_jq_j(I_{k,j}\mid I_{<k})\right)
 =\mathrm{TC}(I_k\mid I_{<k}).
\end{equation}
The minimizers are the true conditional marginals.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Expanding the expected log ratio gives conditional TC plus
\(\sum_j\mathbb E_{I_{<k}}D_{\rm KL}(P(I_{k,j}\mid I_{<k})\|q_j)\).
Each extra KL is nonnegative and vanishes at its conditional marginal.
\end{proof}
For a single shared unbiased bit copied into two positions, the law puts mass
one half on each of 00 and 11. Its best parallel product has a \(\log 2\)-nat
KL gap even though the pair is perfectly reconstructable. Neural parameter
sharing and finite capacity can add error above this lower bound. Reducing TC
by collapsing codes can destroy information, so TC must be evaluated together
with reconstruction, support and hard usage~\citep{wen2026parallel}.

\subsection{Finite capacity and empirical collision ceilings}
\begin{proposition}
If a deterministic codec has at most \(M\) codewords and must exactly reconstruct
\(N>M\) distinct inputs through a deterministic decoder, perfect reconstruction
is impossible. For any fixed codec and input law \(p_s\), the best deterministic
sequence reconstruction probability is
\begin{equation}
 \max_{d}\Pr[d(Q_\psi(X))=X]
   =\sum_i\max_{x:Q_\psi(x)=i}p_s(x),
 \label{eq:collision}
\end{equation}
with empty cells contributing zero.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Pigeonhole requires a collision when \(N>M\). In each code cell the decoder can
select at most one input as its exact reconstruction. Selecting the most likely
member of every nonempty cell attains the sum; no other choice can exceed it.
\end{proof}
The nominal capacity \(\sum_k n_k\log_2V\) bits bounds code cardinality; it is
not an entropy-coded byte size and excludes fallback and metadata. Conversely,
enough nominal codewords does not prevent learned collisions. Equation~\eqref{eq:collision}
explained the completed decoder-replay failure below without relying on a
population capacity shortage.

\subsection{Calibration does not identify arbitrary off-support behavior}
\begin{lemma}
For a finite calibration set \(\mathcal A\) and unrestricted function class,
agreement on \(\mathcal A\) does not determine behavior at an unobserved point
\(x^*\notin\mathcal A\).
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Choose two functions equal on every calibration point but different at \(x^*\).
Their calibration observations coincide, so an observer restricted to those
observations cannot distinguish them.
\end{proof}
This limits guarantees from black-box calibration observations, not all
algorithms with full access to teacher weights. Structural assumptions or
broader tests may supply information beyond that finite sample. The local
studies enumerate their entire small domain, but do not test new domains.

\section{Training Objectives and Hard-Usage Safeguards}
A proposed compiler objective combines masked reconstruction, VQ commitment,
representation alignment, dependence, marginal entropy and a resource penalty:
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal L_{\rm codec}=\mathcal L_{\rm rec}
 +\beta\mathcal L_{\rm commit}+\lambda_A\mathcal L_{\rm align}
 +\lambda_D\mathcal L_{\rm TC}-\lambda_H\mathsf H(q_{\rm marginal})
 +\lambda_R\mathcal R.
\end{equation}
Here \(\mathcal R\) is a proposed cost/rate term; no adaptive resource search was
run. The local implementation has explicit loss coefficients, masked neural
and EMA updates and fixed geometry. The four arms change alignment/dependence
coefficients while executing a common graph. The frozen prior minimizes
\(-\log p_\theta(Q_\psi(x))\). Optional decoder-only replay changes neither
codec assignments nor prior parameters.

Marginal entropy and conditional TC have different roles: a constant code can
have zero TC and terrible fidelity; independent uniform codes may be diverse
but uninformative. Soft assignment entropy can improve while hard usage remains
collapsed. The completed maintained coarse path therefore tracks hard counts
independently, fits initial centroids by deterministic K-means++ and uses
current-batch latent replacements~\citep{arthur2007kmeans}. It schedules
\(\tau_1(a)\) from 2 to 0.25 and \(\beta_1(a)\) from 0 to 0.25 over 200 steps,
and adds a coarse soft marginal-entropy coefficient of 0.05. Quantization stays
hard in the forward pass; there is no Gumbel-Softmax relaxation.

The separate coarse centroid group applies
\(E'_1=E_1+\eta_{\rm coarse}(E_1^{\rm EMA}-E_1)\), with
\(\eta_{\rm coarse}=0.25\). This scale is relative to the EMA centroid proposal,
not to the neural Adam learning rate. Fine EMA and neural rates are unchanged.
Initialization flags, serializable random keys, hard-usage/replacement buffers,
optimizer state and schedule counters are checkpointed. All arms invoke the
same masked maintenance graph, and exact same-backend resume remains tested.
The systems companion gives the detailed state contract~\citep{systemscompanion}.

\section{Falsifiable Next-Stage Evaluation}
\paragraph{Reliable contribution of alignment.}
The immediate scientific question is why AD has not reliably improved over D.
Use new paired student seeds under one fixed teacher/data/sampler protocol;
inspect representation targets and objective scales, and test alignment without
changing the coverage/stability package. Do not select successful seeds after
observing the gate. Decompose the maintenance package with compute-accounted
ablations before attributing the repair to any one component.

\paragraph{External pretrained transfer.}
Only after a held-out real-corpus codec baseline is stable, evaluate multiple
open-weight teacher sizes (for example a Pythia family) and held-out domains
\citep{biderman2023}. Compare scratch NCM, teacher-logit distillation, feature-only
alignment, dependence-only adaptation, copied-but-unverified operators, certified
reuse where possible, and SCALE's full policy. Teacher sizes, tokenizer rules,
calibration quantities and adaptation limits must be fixed before each study.
No such pretrained-size comparison has been completed.

\paragraph{Dependency and capacity.}
Vary serial depth, codebook size and canonical width at matched parameter and
nominal-rate budgets. Measure hard conditional TC, reconstruction, collisions,
output support, teacher-forced/free-prefix errors and decoded output quality.
The exact small-domain surrogate enumerates at most 4096 codewords and cannot
be assumed scalable. Learned density critics or other estimators require
independent checks of bias, variance and optimization behavior.

\paragraph{Cost and failure criteria.}
Record teacher preparation, feature extraction, calibration, architecture search,
all failed adaptation attempts, compilation, updates and I/O. A reused teacher's
sunk pretraining cost should be stated separately from additional adaptation
cost and from a scratch baseline. Reject claims of savings if matched-quality
adaptation cost is not lower. Reject universal canonical alignment if measured
functional residuals or required canonical width grow beyond the resource budget.
If no fixed-budget hierarchy preserves support and useful free-running quality,
report the failed envelope rather than silently increasing it.

\paragraph{Hardware.}
Carry synchronized CPU measurements to actual TPU hardware before claiming TPU
throughput, memory efficiency or multi-host scaling. Match quality, precision,
batch, output lengths, parameter budgets and decoding rules. Report compilation
and steady-state latency separately, with uncertainty, peak memory, collectives
and input/checkpoint overhead. Virtual CPU meshes are correctness checks.

\section{Discussion, Threats to Validity and Future Work}
The completed work moves SCALE from an exclusively conceptual framework to a
small, auditable mechanism-study platform. The collision analysis distinguishes
recoverable information at the input from distinctions retained by the codec.
Balanced sampling restored coverage; maintenance stabilized both hard coarse
codes. Neither result proves that aligned teacher features improve a parallel
prior, and the final negative alignment gate must guide the next experiment.

The evidence has one teacher, one finite support, fixed datasets and changing
student seed cohorts across follow-ups. Split names denote independent draws,
not unseen sequences. Paired arms support within-protocol contrasts; differing
cohorts, samplers and maintenance settings confound cross-protocol mean changes.
The 20-model stability result is conditional on this domain and the combined
package. Compiler-estimated step FLOPs and local gates are not measured total
compute or externally preregistered confirmation. The text fixture overfit.

The conditional propositions describe assumptions that a real compiler must
verify; they are not certificates supplied by PCA. Different widths may lack a
common invariant functional subspace. Tokenizers, attention patterns and
normalization can defeat exact transplantation. Hierarchical sampling can
amplify local errors, and hard quantization can remove low-probability states.
The present deterministic output decoder is a documented evaluation protocol,
not a calibrated general language distribution.

Future work includes real-corpus held-out tests, maintenance component ablations,
robust alignment controls, scalable dependence estimation, cross-size learned
interfaces, selective operator transplantation and complete adaptation accounting.
Prompt/length/EOS interfaces, serialized mixed byte routing, Rust orchestration,
TPU profiling and multi-host recovery remain systems work. A useful SCALE result
must demonstrate fidelity and cost together under declared gates.

\section{Conclusion}
SCALE remains a conditional, falsifiable proposal for cross-size representation
compilation. Completed CPU engineering and finite-domain controls now supply a
reproducible testbed, collision diagnosis and a coarse-codebook stabilization
result across \StabilityModels{} new-seed models. The reliable alignment gate
still failed despite stable usage and support. These outcomes refine the next
experiments without establishing pretrained transfer, language-model quality,
compute savings or TPU performance.
